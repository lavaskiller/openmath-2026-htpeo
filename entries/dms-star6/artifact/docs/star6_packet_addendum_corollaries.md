# Addendum to the star6 packet (draft v3): unconditional corollaries in plain vocabulary

Status 2026-10-02. Supplements §2 of `star6_packet_draft_v3.md` (which is unchanged). Proposed as **§2.1a
"Artifact A′ — corollaries (pack5)"**. DMS is **not** proved; everything below is unconditional and kernel-checked.

## What it adds
Artifact A (`RH2F.layer37`) states its unconditional parts in project vocabulary (BASE12, SIMPLE14, B14D, FEEXIST16:
2-cut-reduced graphs, EX1-goodness, far-exchange sets). Artifact A′ derives from the same library, with no new
mathematics, statements a reader can check against the conjecture directly. Lean 4.33.1 + Mathlib v4.33.1, four
modules on top of artifact A (`pack5/src`: `Star6Bounded`, `Star6Corollaries`, `Star6Equiv`, `Star6Simple`), no
`sorry`, no `axiom`, no `native_decide`; every theorem depends on `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` only.

## Main statements (names in namespace `Star6`)
1. **Small order.**
   - `star6_cubic_bridgeless_le14`: every bridgeless cubic loopless multigraph with at most **14** vertices is star
     6-edge-colourable.
   - `matching_colour_class_10_14`: in a connected one with 10–14 vertices, every edge lies in (and is avoided by) a
     perfect matching that is a colour class of a star 6-edge-colouring.
   - `star6_leaf_le14`: all leaf graphs T(P, g) of connected bridgeless cubic multigraphs `P` with ≤ 14 vertices are
     star 6-edge-colourable.
   - `star6_subcubic_le7`: DMS for all loopless subcubic multigraphs with at most **7** vertices.
   - `bounded_reduction`: for every `N`, the chain's hypotheses (H), (II_D) restricted to order ≤ N imply the above
     for order ≤ N (≤ N/2 for general subcubic graphs) — the induction never looks at larger graphs.
   The same in `Sym2`/`Fintype` vocabulary (`plain_…`) and for Mathlib `SimpleGraph` (`simple_…`).
2. **Shape of a counterexample.**
   - `dms_of_hyp16_iid16`: DMS follows from two statements about 2-cut-reduced connected bridgeless cubic multigraphs
     on **at least 16** vertices (EX1-good; leaf graphs at eligible edges colourable).
   - `counterexample_cubic16` / `dms_iff_cubic16`: DMS holds **iff** every connected bridgeless cubic loopless
     multigraph on ≥ 16 vertices and each of its leaf graphs is star 6-edge-colourable.
   - `smallest_not_ex1`: a smallest connected bridgeless cubic multigraph that is not EX1-good is 2-cut-reduced and has
     ≥ 16 vertices; `minimal_counterexample`: an edge-minimal counterexample lives on ≥ 8 vertices, is connected, and if
     cubic is bridgeless with ≥ 16 vertices.
3. **Known theorems** (by-products; absent from Mathlib v4.33.1): Schönberger's theorem and Petersen's theorem
   (connected case) for multigraphs and for `SimpleGraph` (`schoenberger_in/out`, `petersen`, `plain_schoenberger`,
   `simple_schoenberger`, `simple_petersen_connected`). See `star6_m2_candidates.md`.
4. **Re-exports** of the reductions of artifact A under readable names (`dms_of_H_II`, `dms_of_H_IID'`,
   `dms_of_I_II'`, `ex1_of_H`, `dms_of_HOLE`, `dms_iff_plain`, `chain_top`).

## Suggested wording for the result summary
"Unconditionally (Lean-checked): every bridgeless cubic loopless multigraph on at most 14 vertices, each leaf graph of
a connected one, and every loopless subcubic multigraph on at most 7 vertices is star 6-edge-colourable; and the
Dvořák–Mohar–Šámal conjecture is equivalent to star 6-edge-colourability of the connected bridgeless cubic multigraphs
on at least 16 vertices and their leaf graphs. A smallest bridgeless cubic graph violating the strengthened property
EX1 is 2-cut-reduced and has at least 16 vertices."

## Honest limits (to keep in the packet)
- The bound for general subcubic graphs is 7, not 14 (the reduction for two vertices of degree < 3 doubles the graph).
- "A minimum counterexample is cubic" is not proved; the correct statement is the equivalence above (cubic graphs *and
  their leaf graphs*).
- Nothing unconditional at 16 vertices beyond `FEEXIST16` and the classification `cls16c`.
- The proofs of the bounded theorems are the library's proofs re-run with bounded hypotheses (generated by `gen5.py`,
  every replacement listed); the mathematical content is that of artifact A.
